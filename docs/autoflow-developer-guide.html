AutoFlow — how the workshop runs

AutoFlow takes a car from the moment it arrives at the workshop to the moment the customer drives it away — and tracks every job, part, price and payment along the way. This guide explains the whole thing in plain language. No technical terms.

1What is AutoFlow?

Think of it as a checklist that the whole workshop shares.

A car comes in for repair. Lots of people touch it — the mechanic, the service advisor, the admin, the manager. Lots of things happen — photos, diagnosis, supplier quotes, customer approval, the actual repair, payment, delivery.

Without a system, all of that lives in people's heads, in WhatsApp chats, on paper. Things get forgotten. AutoFlow puts the entire process on one screen so everyone can see exactly where a car is and what happens next.

One simple idea: every car becomes a "job". Every job moves through the same 20 steps, in order, from "car arrived" to "customer happy". Anyone can open a job and instantly see what's done and what's left.

2Who uses it

Five kinds of people. Each has their part of the job.

🔧 Bay Team (the mechanics)
Receive the car, take photos, diagnose the problem, choose the parts needed, do the actual repair, and tick the quality checks.
📞 Service Advisor
The customer's main contact. Creates the WhatsApp group, sends the price approval, asks for feedback at the end.
🗂️ Admin
Handles the office side — customer details, supplier prices, the markup, quotation, invoice and recording payment.
👔 Management
Keeps an eye on pricing and signs off the quality check before a car can be handed back.
👑 Super Admin (the owner)
Can do everything. Mostly watches the Finance and Profit screens to see how the business is doing.

And of course — the customer. They never log in, but they get WhatsApp messages, approve their quote with one tap, and leave feedback at the end.

4The 8 areas of work

20 steps is a lot to look at. So we group them into 8 friendly areas.

When you open a car's job, you don't see a wall of 20 steps. You see 8 areas down the side — like chapters in a book. Click an area, and you see just the steps that belong to it. Each area shows its own progress (like "2 of 3 done").

🧾1. Check-in
Car arrives, complaints noted, customer details taken.
🚗2. Vehicle
Photos of the car body and the chassis number.
💬3. Communication
A WhatsApp group with the customer and the team.
🩺4. Diagnosis
Find the problem, list the parts needed.
💰5. Estimate

The simple version: Get the car in → find the problem → agree a price → do the work → hand it back. The 8 areas are just those 5 ideas, spelled out a little more.

5The 20 steps, explained simply

Each step is one clear action. Here's what happens at each — with a real example. The example car is WXY 1234 — Ahmad's Honda Civic.

🧾 Check-in

Getting the car into the system.

1
Create the job
Done by Bay or Service Advisor

The moment a car arrives — before anyone even talks to the customer — staff type the car's plate number into AutoFlow. The system instantly creates a job with its own reference code.

ExampleMohd types "WXY 1234". AutoFlow creates job WF-2024-0001. The car is now officially "in the system".
2
Inspection sheet
Done by Bay

The mechanic walks around the car with the customer, ticks off a quick checklist (exterior, interior, any existing dents), and notes down what the customer is complaining about.

ExampleNote written: "Customer says brakes squeak in the morning, aircon blowing warm. Mileage 45,230 km."
5
Customer details
Done by Admin or Service Advisor

Admin fills in the customer's full information — name, phone, email, car model, mileage, chassis number. This is needed later to send the price approval and the receipt.

ExampleName: Ahmad bin Abdullah · Phone: +60 12-345 6789 · Car: Honda Civic 2020.
🚗 Vehicle

Proof of the car's condition.

4
Vehicle photos
Done by Bay

The mechanic photographs the car from four sides (front, back, left, right) and takes a clear photo of the chassis number. This protects both the workshop and the customer — everyone agrees on the car's condition before any work begins.

Example5 photos uploaded. Chassis number read off the photo and confirmed: MH1234567890.
💬 Communication

Keeping the customer in the loop.

3
WhatsApp group
Done by Service Advisor

A WhatsApp group is created with the customer, the service advisor and the mechanic. All repair updates and photos go here. AutoFlow even writes the group name and the invite message for you — one tap opens WhatsApp ready to go.

ExampleGroup created: "WF-2024-0001 - WXY 1234 Repair" with Ahmad, Sarah and Mohd.
🩺 Diagnosis

Working out what's wrong and what's needed.

6
Troubleshooting
Done by Bay

The mechanic inspects the car and writes down what they found and what they recommend fixing.

Example"Front brake pads worn out. AC compressor not kicking in. Engine oil overdue. Recommend: replace brake pads, recharge AC + new cabin filter, full oil service."
7
Spare parts needed
Done by Bay

The mechanic picks the parts needed from a built-in catalogue — by category, one tap each — and sets quantities. AutoFlow then writes a tidy "parts enquiry" message that can be sent straight to suppliers on WhatsApp to ask for prices.

ExampleParts chosen: Brake Pads (Front), Engine Oil 5W-30, Air Filter, AC Gas, Oil Filter. AutoFlow generates the enquiry message → sent to 3 suppliers.
💰 Estimate

Turning supplier prices into a customer price.

8
Supplier pricing
Done by Admin or Management

Suppliers reply on WhatsApp with their prices. The admin types each supplier's prices into AutoFlow. Because every supplier is kept separate, the workshop can compare them side by side and see who's cheapest.

ExampleBrake pads: Suan Huat RM 150, Stuttgart RM 160, Bavaria RM 155. Suan Huat is cheapest.
9
Markup
Done by Admin or Management

The workshop adds its profit margin on top of the supplier price. AutoFlow checks that every part keeps at least a 60% profit margin — if something is below, it warns you in red.

ExampleBrake pads cost RM 150 → sold to customer at RM 375. That's a 60% margin. ✅
✅ Authorization

Getting the customer's "yes" before spending their money.

10
Customer confirmation
Done by Service Advisor

AutoFlow sends the customer a secure link. The customer opens it on their phone — no app, no login — and for each part chooses Original or cheaper alternative, then taps Approve. The workshop knows immediately.

ExampleAhmad opens the link, picks Original parts (he wants the longer warranty), taps Approve RM 2,145.
11
Quotation
Done by Admin

A proper quotation document is built — all the parts, labour charge, tax, and grand total — and saved as a PDF that can be shared with the customer.

ExampleQuotation: parts RM 2,145 + labour RM 500 + tax = grand total. PDF generated.
🔧 Parts & Repair

Ordering the parts and doing the actual work.

12
Order the parts
Done by Admin

This is the workshop's pricing command-centre. The admin sees every part's price from every supplier in one table, picks the best supplier for each, and AutoFlow writes a separate WhatsApp order message for each supplier. There's also a summary showing the cheapest supplier and the total customer charge.

ExampleCrank Sensor → ordered from Suan Huat. Ignition Coil → from Stuttgart. AutoFlow sends each supplier their own order list on WhatsApp.
13
Parts arrive
Done by Bay

When parts arrive, the mechanic checks each one off — confirming the right quantity arrived and it's in good condition. If anything is damaged or wrong, AutoFlow flags it so the supplier can be contacted.

ExampleAll 3 ordered parts arrived. Mohd marks each "Received — Good condition".
14
Do the work & mark complete
Done by Bay

The mechanic does the repair, uploads "before / during / after" photos as proof of the work, writes completion notes, and marks the work done.

ExampleBrake pads replaced, AC recharged, oil changed. 3 photos uploaded. Note: "All work complete, no surprises."
15
Quality check
Done by Management

Management runs a final quality checklist — repairs done properly, test drive done, nothing else wrong, car ready. They either approve it, or send it back for rework.

ExampleAll checks pass. QC approved. The car is now Ready for pickup.
🧼 Close-out

Handing the car back and finishing up.

16
Car wash
Done by Bay

The car gets a final wash and clean before it goes back to the customer — exterior wash, interior vacuum, windows, tyre shine.

17
Send the receipt
Done by Admin

AutoFlow builds the final receipt — all the parts, labour, tax and total — and writes it as a neat message that can be edited and sent to the customer on WhatsApp. It even includes a reminder for the next service date.

ExampleReceipt total RM 1,398.60 sent to Ahmad. Reminder: next inspection 22 Nov 2026.
18
Receive payment
Done by Admin

The admin records how the customer paid — cash, card, online transfer or QR — the amount, and a reference number.

ExampleAhmad pays by QR (DuitNow). Admin records it against the job.
19
Hand over the car
Done by Admin or Service Advisor

A final handover checklist — ID verified, payment confirmed, customer happy with the car, documents handed over, signature taken. Then the keys go back.

20
Ask for feedback
Done by Service Advisor

AutoFlow sends the customer a short feedback link on WhatsApp. The customer rates the service out of 5 stars and leaves a comment. Once that's in, the job is officially closed.

ExampleAhmad leaves 5★: "Excellent service, brakes feel new, car came back clean." Job closed. ✅

6The job board

One screen to see every car in the workshop right now.

The job board is like a whiteboard with columns — Intake, Diagnosis, Awaiting, In Repair, QC, Ready. Every car sits in the column that matches its stage. One glance tells you how busy the workshop is and which cars need attention.

Each car shows as a small card: the plate number, the customer, which step it's on, and the estimated total. Click a card to open that car's full job.

The dashboard takes this even further — it shows the day's numbers at a glance: how many active jobs, how many waiting for customer approval, how many ready for pickup, and today's revenue.

7Parts & pricing

Every part comes in two grades. The customer chooses.

AutoFlow keeps a catalogue of parts, organised by category (brakes, engine, electrical, AC, suspension). Every part is offered in two grades:

Original (ORI)
The genuine manufacturer part. Comes with a 1-year warranty. Costs more — best for newer cars.
Alternative (OM)
A good-quality non-branded part. Comes with a 6-month warranty. Around 25–35% cheaper.

When the customer approves their quote (Step 10), they choose Original or Alternative for each part — so they're always in control of the cost-versus-warranty trade-off.

Staff can also add new categories and new parts to the catalogue at any time.

8Finance & profit

Three things: what the workshop paid, what the owners spent, and how much profit each car made.

💼 Company expenses

For every repair, the workshop pays suppliers for the parts. The admin uploads the bank payment slip for each part, and AutoFlow reads the amount off it automatically (the admin can correct it if needed). Add them all up and you have the real cost of that repair.

Note: the bank slip files are never kept. AutoFlow only reads the amount and then discards the file. Nothing sensitive is stored.

👤 Personal expenses

The two owners also have their own spending to track. Each uploads their bank statement, and AutoFlow sorts every transaction into one of 7 categories. If it can't tell which category a transaction belongs to, it puts it in "Others" with a yellow warning, so the owner can fix it with one tap.

Each owner can edit only their own spending, but both can see the combined totals.

📈 Profit calculator

This is where it all comes together. For each finished car, AutoFlow compares:

  • What the customer paid (the quoted price, with markup) — the revenue.
  • What the workshop actually paid suppliers (from the bank slips) — the cost.

The difference is the profit. AutoFlow shows it per car and even per part, with a colour code — green if the margin is healthy, red if it needs a look. The owner can finally see, with proof, exactly how much each repair earned.

In short: the workflow captures what you charged, the company-expenses tab captures what you paid, and the profit calculator does the maths. No more guessing.

9Full example — Ahmad's Honda Civic

The whole journey, start to finish, in one story.

Ahmad brings his Honda Civic (WXY 1234) in on a Friday morning. His complaint: squeaky brakes, warm aircon, and the oil is overdue.
Friday morning — the car comes in
  1. Mohd (mechanic) types the plate into AutoFlow. Job WF-2024-0001 is created.
  2. He walks around the car with Ahmad, ticks the inspection checklist, and notes the complaints.
  3. Sarah (service advisor) starts a WhatsApp group with Ahmad, herself and Mohd.
  4. Mohd photographs the car from all sides and the chassis number.
  5. Admin fills in Ahmad's full details. The car's stage moves to Diagnosis.
Friday midday — figuring out the price
  1. Mohd diagnoses the faults and picks the parts needed from the catalogue.
  2. AutoFlow writes a parts enquiry — Mohd sends it to three suppliers on WhatsApp.
  3. Suppliers reply with prices through the afternoon. Admin types them all in.
  4. Admin adds the markup. Every part keeps a healthy 60% margin. The quote is ready.
Friday afternoon — the customer decides
  1. Sarah sends Ahmad a secure approval link. The car's stage moves to Awaiting.
  2. Ahmad opens it on his phone, picks Original parts for the warranty, and taps Approve.
  3. Admin builds the formal quotation PDF.
Saturday — the repair
  1. Admin orders the parts — picking the cheapest supplier for each. Stage: In Repair.
  2. Parts arrive by late morning. Mohd checks each one in.
  3. Mohd does the repair and uploads before / during / after photos.
  4. Management runs the quality check — all good. The car is now Ready.
Saturday afternoon — handing back
  1. The car is washed and cleaned.
  2. Admin sends Ahmad the receipt on WhatsApp.
  3. Ahmad pays by QR. Admin records the payment.
  4. Sarah does the handover checklist; Ahmad signs and collects his keys.
  5. Sarah sends a feedback link. Ahmad leaves 5 stars. The job is closed.
The following week — the numbers
  1. Admin uploads the supplier bank slips into the Finance section.
  2. AutoFlow reads the real amounts paid.
  3. The Profit Calculator now shows it clearly: this repair earned the workshop a healthy profit — and the owner can see exactly how much.
That's AutoFlow. One shared system, from the plate scan to the profit report — so nothing is forgotten, the customer always knows what's happening, and the owner always knows how the business is doing.
